Common Questions

Got Questions?

Can I use this address for SEC/BIR registration?
Yes. All plans include address-use agreements and premises documents accepted for business registration purposes in the Philippines.
Where exactly is the address located?
Our virtual office is located at SGM Virtual Office, Mabuhay IT Park, General Santos City — a recognized commercial business address.
How will I know when my mail arrives?
On the Plus and Premium plans, we notify you by message as soon as mail or parcels are received on your behalf.
Can I book the meeting room separately?
The 3 hours of conference room use is included in the Premium plan. Additional hours may be available — contact us for rates.
Is there a minimum commitment period?
Plans are billed monthly with no long-term lock-in. You can upgrade, downgrade, or cancel with proper notice.
Is my business information kept confidential?
Absolutely. All client information and correspondence is handled with strict confidentiality by our trained team.
